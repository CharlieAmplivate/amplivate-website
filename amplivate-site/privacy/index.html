Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Who we are

Amplivate ("we", "us", "our") is a digital marketing agency based in West Sussex and operating UK-wide. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why, and what rights you have over it. Our contact email is hello@amplivate.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Information you submit through our contact form: name, email address, business name, and message content.
  • Basic technical data collected automatically, such as pages visited, if analytics are enabled.

Why we collect it

We use contact form submissions to respond to enquiries and provide quotes or strategy calls. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Legal basis

We process contact form data on the basis of legitimate interest (responding to a direct enquiry you've made) under UK GDPR.

How long we keep it

We keep contact form submissions for up to 12 months and then delete them, unless you become a client, in which case they form part of your client records.

Third parties

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Contact form submissions are processed via Web3Forms, which forwards them to our inbox. We do not share your data beyond what's needed to respond to your enquiry.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies. We use your browser's local storage to remember whether you chose light or dark mode. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies at this time. If that changes, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact hello@amplivate.co.uk.
