Never lose a job to a missed call. The second a call goes unanswered, your customer gets an instant text back with a way to book, so the enquiry doesn't go to a competitor.

Benefits

  • Fewer lost leads
  • Instant response
  • No extra effort

Why it matters

When a call goes unanswered, most people don't leave a voicemail. They ring the next business on the list. An instant text back keeps the conversation going, gives the customer an easy way to book, and means a busy day on the job doesn't cost you work.

Who it's for

  • Trades and service businesses who can't answer the phone mid job
  • Busy practices and shops where calls get missed at peak times
  • Anyone who has lost a job because the customer rang someone else
