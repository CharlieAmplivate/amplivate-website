Overview

Most happy customers never leave a review, not because they don't want to, but because nobody asked them at the right time. We set up automatic, on-brand follow-up that asks at the right moment — without sounding like a robot.

Benefits

  • More reviews from customers who were already happy
  • Follow-up that happens automatically, with no manual chasing
  • Requests and responses that sound like your business, not a template

What's included

  • Automated review request follow-up
  • On-brand messaging tailored to your business
  • Monthly reporting on review volume and rating
Why it matters

What this does for your business.

Reviews play a big part in whether a customer picks you, and they help your Google ranking too. Most happy customers are willing to leave one; they just need asking at the right moment. Automating that ask means your review count grows steadily without you chasing anyone.

Who it's for
  • Businesses with happy customers but only a handful of Google reviews
  • Anyone who forgets to ask for reviews at the end of a job
  • Businesses whose competitors have far more reviews than they do

FAQs

Common questions.

Will this feel like spam to my customers?

No — requests are timed and worded to feel like a genuine follow-up, not a mass blast.

What happens if a customer leaves negative feedback?

Negative feedback is flagged to you immediately, so you can respond fast, put things right, and keep the customer happy.
