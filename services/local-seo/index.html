We optimise your website, on page content, local citations and Google Business Profile structure so you show up for the searches that matter in your area. Built for businesses competing locally, or taking local SEO seriously for the first time.

Benefits

  • Higher visibility in local search results and Google Maps
  • More enquiries from people who are ready to buy
  • A clear picture of which searches actually bring you customers

Why it matters

When someone needs a local service, they search for it and pick from the first few businesses Google shows them, usually in the map results. If you're not there, those customers go to whoever is. Local SEO is how you earn that spot: a technically sound website, content that matches what people in your area actually search for, and consistent business details across the web so Google trusts you.

Who it's for

  • Trades and local service businesses competing for "near me" searches
  • Businesses that rely on customers from a particular town or area
  • Anyone whose competitors keep appearing above them on Google Maps
