About

An agency, built the way we'd want to hire one.

Why Amplivate exists

Too often we see local business marketing sold as a package instead of built as a strategy — the same SEO checklist, the same templated website, the same generic social posts, regardless of what the business actually needs. Amplivate exists to do it differently: work built around the business in front of us, priced fairly, and reported on honestly.

Meet the founder

Charlie

Founder & Managing Director

I started Amplivate to give local businesses the kind of joined-up marketing that usually needs a much bigger budget: a Google presence that brings in enquiries, a website built to convert, and simple automations that stop leads slipping through the cracks. Based in West Sussex and working with businesses across the UK, I'm the person running your campaigns, building your site and answering your emails.

Our mission

To give local UK businesses access to the kind of strategic, bespoke marketing usually reserved for companies with much bigger budgets — without the bloated retainers or the corporate fluff.

Values

Honesty over hype

No invented urgency, no inflated promises, no results we can't stand behind.

Fit over templates

A plan built around your business and the market you actually compete in.

Access over hierarchy

You deal directly with the person doing the work.

What makes us different

Most agencies scale by adding account managers between you and the work. Amplivate is built to stay small on purpose — every client works directly with the person running their campaigns, building their site, and answering their emails.

How we help businesses grow

Through the work that actually moves the needle for local businesses — search visibility, a Google Business Profile that converts, a website built to sell, and social media that keeps you visible — plus practical AI tools that handle repetitive follow-up so nothing falls through the cracks.

Vision for the future

As Amplivate grows, so does this: a client dashboard for real-time reporting, AI tools clients can manage themselves, and resources for local businesses trying to understand their own marketing. Added because it's useful, not because it looks impressive.

Privacy Policy
Last updated: 30 September 2026

  

    
Who we are
Amplivate is a digital marketing agency run by Charlie Robinson, based in West Sussex and working with businesses across the UK. Charlie Robinson is responsible for any personal data collected through this website ("we", "us", "our"). You can contact us about anything in this policy at hello@amplivate.co.uk.

    
What we collect
  • Contact form enquiries: your name, email address, business name (if you give it), what you're enquiring about, and your message.
  • Technical data: when you visit, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type, which it uses to deliver and secure the site.

    
Why we use it
We use your enquiry to reply to you, arrange a strategy call if you ask for one, and put together a quote or plan. We don't use it for marketing mailing lists, and we never sell your data.

    
Legal basis
Under UK GDPR we rely on legitimate interests to process your enquiry: you've contacted us and asked for a reply. If you go on to become a client, we process your details as needed to carry out our agreement with you.

    
How long we keep it
We keep contact form enquiries for up to 12 months and then delete them, unless you become a client, in which case they form part of your client records for as long as we work together and as required for tax and accounting purposes.

    
Who we share it with
We only use trusted services that are needed to run this website:
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and processes the technical data described above.
  • Web3Forms receives contact form submissions and forwards them to our inbox.
These providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on recognised legal safeguards for international transfers. We don't share your data with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

    
Cookies and browser storage
This website doesn't use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. It stores two small settings in your own browser: whether you chose light or dark mode, and a temporary marker used for the page transition animation. These stay on your device, are never sent to us, and are only there to make the site work as you'd expect. If we ever add analytics, we'll update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

    
Your rights
You have the right to ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and to ask us to correct it, delete it, or stop or limit how we use it. You can also object to how we use it. To make a request, email hello@amplivate.co.uk and we'll respond within one month.

    
Complaints
If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.

    
Changes to this policy
We'll update this page if anything changes, and the date at the top will show when it was last revised.
